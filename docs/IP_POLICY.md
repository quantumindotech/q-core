# Intellectual Property and Licensing Policy

## Rights-first policy

Q-CORE must not claim ownership of material merely because it appears in this repository. Authorship, copyright ownership, permission to use, and license compatibility must be established from evidence.

## Repository licensing status

**Repository-wide licensing is not yet declared.** A license must not be applied retroactively to material whose provenance or rights are unknown.

Before selecting and publishing a repository license:
1. inventory all files and dependencies;
2. verify authorship and origin;
3. identify third-party license obligations and notices;
4. confirm contributor permissions or assignments where required;
5. document the scope of the chosen license.

AGPL-3.0-only may be evaluated for eligible original code if the rights holders approve it and all incorporated material is compatible. This is a candidate for review, not a license declaration or a claim that all repository material is eligible.

## Third-party and AI-assisted material

Third-party components remain under their applicable upstream terms. AI-assisted material requires human review, provenance checks where source material is involved, and compatibility review. Unknown provenance is a release blocker.

## No implied affiliation

References to external organizations, projects, standards, or communities are informational only and do not imply endorsement, sponsorship, certification, membership-based approval, or affiliation.
