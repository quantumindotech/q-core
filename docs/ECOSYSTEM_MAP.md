# Ecosystem Map

Q-CORE coordinates architecture and interfaces; it does not absorb the identity, governance, ownership, or release authority of related projects.

## Core architectural line

- Q-CORE: master architecture and integration controller.
- UQY: proposed network, ledger, consensus, and execution layer.
- UNY: proposed wallet, identity/access, authorization, custody, and recovery layer.
- UTY: proposed native utility coin for gas and settlement.
- QMC: separate master-coin and Layer #0 research line.

These are architectural definitions and targets, not assertions of completed deployment.

## Distinct tokenization line

Trisula is a separate tokenization project family:
- QSDT
- QSDC
- QSDG
- QTM — Trisula master token; distinct from QMC and Q-CORE.
